# Offer Doc Outline

What a good offer document should contain so the funnel can be filled in one pass.
The client can write this as a Google Doc, a text file, or just answer these
questions in chat. Anything missing becomes a `TODO:` in the funnel.

## 1. Brand basics
- Brand / business name
- Logo file (SVG or PNG)
- Booking link (Cal.com or similar)
- Support email
- Website URL

## 2. Audience & offer
- Who is this for? (the avatar — role, situation)
- Core outcome they get (the "X")
- Timeframe (the "Y")
- The unique mechanism / method (what makes it different)
- The #1 objection you remove

## 3. Problem framing (for the 3 cards)
- **Old Way** — how they've been doing it
- **Problem** — why that fails / isn't their fault
- **New Way** — the better approach you offer

## 4. Proof
- **Case study #1**: client name (if allowed), starting problem, what they tried,
  what you did, the result, final headline result
- **Case study #2**: same structure
- **3 short testimonials**: for each — a one-line result headline, the customer
  name, and 1–2 sentences of review

## 5. Your story (Authority section)
- The low point / struggle
- The turning point / big realization
- The transformation / result
- Why you created this offer

## 6. FAQs
- 6 common questions + your answers (objections, requirements, differentiation,
  time, "will it work for me", "is this legit")

## 7. Trust details
- Guarantee terms (e.g. "30-day money back")
- Customer / client count (only if real)

## 8. Videos (optional)
- Hero VSL embed URL
- Case-study video embed URLs
- Call-booked page: welcome video + resource video URLs

## 9. Call-booked page (optional overrides)
- Any custom pre-call steps (otherwise the generic 3 steps are used)
- Resource links to show before the call
